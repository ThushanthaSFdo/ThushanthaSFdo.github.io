## Background

This space shooter game was developed using Swift to provide an engaging and immersive experience for players. Inspired by classic arcade shooters, it combines modern graphics and intuitive controls to create a dynamic gameplay environment. Players must strategically manage their ship's energy and health while navigating through waves of enemies, enhancing their skills and reflexes. The game aims to deliver not only entertainment but also a sense of achievement as players strive to reach high scores and unlock new challenges. With its captivating design and addictive mechanics, this app is designed for both casual gamers and space enthusiasts alike.
