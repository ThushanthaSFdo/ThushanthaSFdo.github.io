## Background

This Tic Tac Toe game was developed to provide a modern twist on the timeless classic. Designed for two players, it emphasizes friendly competition and strategic thinking. The app tracks scores for each session, allowing players to see who reigns supreme. Its user-friendly interface and smooth gameplay make it accessible to everyone, fostering social interaction and enjoyment.

## The future

Future improvements includes;
- **Single Player Game Option:** Introduce an AI opponent for solo play, allowing users to enjoy the game even when friends are unavailable.
- **Score History and Player History:** Implement a feature to track scores over multiple sessions, providing players with insights into their performance trends.
- **Player Profiles:** Create customizable player profiles that store individual statistics, preferences, and achievements, enhancing user engagement and personalization.
